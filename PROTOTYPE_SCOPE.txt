RareBridge — prototype scope proposed for approval
Prepared 4 October 2026. Planning document only; implementation has not started.
This proposal supersedes earlier feature wishlists, not the stable handoff API.
Budget baseline: the previously reported 17 hours, four people. Rebase the schedule
against actual hours remaining when approval arrives.

PRODUCT PROMISE
Help a patient organization find an existing research resource, understand the
conditions for its particular proposed use, and approach an appropriate partner
with a sourced collaboration brief.

Main user: patient-organization leader. Primary goal: prepare a natural-history
study, which follows how a condition changes over time. Starting conditions:
STXBP1-, SYNGAP1- and SCN2A-related disorders. These are demonstration cases,
not gene-wide compatibility categories. The challenge explicitly permits a
focused disease cluster.

COMMITTED CONTENT BREADTH
- Three disease records, their genes, selected mechanisms/processes and HPO
  phenotype terms, stable identifiers and synonyms. Retain the imported graph
  for inspection; select a compact useful route for the default display.
- Six resource/program records: the published STARR/ProMMiS framework; STARR;
  ProMMiS; SCN2A Clinical Trial Readiness Study; Inchstone outcome-measure
  adaptation; Simons Searchlight longitudinal research-data access route.
  The framework and its component studies are connected explicitly. They are
  not presented as six independent discoveries or independent supporting papers.
  A program record is not described as a downloadable validated instrument.
- Four scoped target/resource assessments: framework for STXBP1; framework for
  SYNGAP1; possible framework adaptation for SCN2A; Simons Searchlight data access
  for a proposed SYNGAP1 natural-history project. Existing two bundles seed this.
  Other cards have source-backed metadata and a clear 'not assessed for this use'
  state; never attach a different disease's assessment to them.
- Three verified patient-organization routes plus relevant study-team/shared-
  initiative public routes. Link investigators only when sources establish their
  role. A listed author is not automatically the current asset owner.
- A small reviewed overlay sufficient for each displayed route and check.
  Planning cap: roughly 10-15 decisive source pages/papers and 20-30 reviewed
  assertions; these are effort bounds, not verified-content counts today.
  Broader imported claims stay pending; source-count inflation is prohibited.
- One two-variant SCN2A context card from one primary experimental paper is a
  time-boxed enhancement: preserve variant, experimental model and measured
  effect. Include only if reviewed by hour 6. No universal SCN2A gain/loss label.
- Existing imported model/dataset metadata may remain inspectable as unreviewed
  records, but no lab-model reuse assessment or therapeutic workflow is promised.

FOUR USER VIEWS
1. Find your starting point.
   One search accepts disease/gene names, known synonyms, selected symptoms,
   mechanisms and curated organizations. All resolve into the same disease
   journey. Plain-language summary, exact community and collection coverage are
   visible. Unknown queries produce a helpful empty state.
2. Understand connections and existing resources.
   Compact graph plus readable path cards: disease -> gene/mechanism/phenotype
   -> related condition -> study/resource -> organization/investigator.
   Compute candidate relationships from recorded shared graph features. State
   the actual feature, source and review status. Published connections and
   inferred research leads are separate labels, not separate applications.
   Broad shared symptoms provide weaker clues than specific documented links.
   Rankings prioritize reviewed, goal-relevant routes and expose the reason;
   they do not represent clinical probability. Show six resource/program cards
   with type, purpose, source, known access conditions and assessed/unassessed
   status. Related resources remain available without implying suitability.
3. Check this resource for this proposed use.
   Show disease, subgroup/proposed use as documented in the bundle and one goal.
   Four assessment bundles answer recorded disease coverage, measurement/cohort
   fit, access/materials and local expertise/equipment questions. Fixed curated
   cases prevent arbitrary form changes from silently retaining old evidence.
   Expose supported, refuted, conflicting, unknown and unreviewed checks. Each
   missing check has a concrete next question and responsible collaborator role.
   A source drawer shows original URL, publication/retrieval dates where known,
   narrow assertion, review status and evidence/model context. Hide/reset one
   publication across DOI/PMID aliases to show dependency changes. Explain a
   changed check even if the overall outcome remains 'needs information'.
4. Prepare the next action.
   Editable collaboration brief with resource, proposed use, explained route,
   known differences, supporting sources, required open checks, public partner
   routes, next research milestone and draft message. Export as plain text or
   browser print/save PDF. Export the currently displayed assessment, including
   withheld sources. The app prepares a message for review; it does not send it.

AI CAPABILITIES INCLUDED
Actual OpenAI integration is part of the core, because challenge-track prizes
require it. Use schema-constrained extraction from a supplied public abstract or
source excerpt with its identifier/URL. Validate IDs, citations, snippets and
allowed relationships; show the source-linked candidate claims in a small
evidence-import panel, marked pending. Reconcile known names to existing IDs.
Generate a plain-language explanation and draft brief using the deterministic
assessment and provided sources. The model cannot promote scientific assertions
or overwrite check outcomes. Schema compliance does not establish factual truth.
Record model, request/response provenance and source version/hash as available.
Cache genuine successful responses and label live/cached/unavailable states.
Required demo: one real extraction preview and one grounded explanation.
No unrestricted biomedical chatbot, arbitrary URL crawler or autonomous agent.

WHAT WE REUSE
1. Local DisMech snapshot, graph builder, canonical IDs and metadata. Keep
   Monarch/DisMech attribution, pinned commit and CC-BY-4.0 content terms.
2. Our existing assessment.py source/check/alias/withdrawal logic and software
   tests. Candidate matching remains separate from applicability assessment.
3. Existing official study/program pages for asset metadata and public routes.
   Simons/SFARI institutional affiliation, agreement and IRB approval/exemption
   are useful documented access checks. Data suitability still needs review.
4. The patient-advocate/researcher collaboration and measurement-adaptation
   workflow already demonstrated by Inchstone. Credit that effort and connect
   users to it; do not claim we invented cross-condition measure adaptation.
5. HPO/MONDO identifiers already present and transparent shared-term matching,
   rather than a fresh ontology pipeline or foundation-model training project.
6. Current OpenAI Structured Outputs guidance for bounded extraction.

Reuse means metadata, permitted data/code and useful workflow patterns. It does
not mean copying proprietary product code, restricted participant data or
copyrighted measurement instruments. Current availability/recruitment is stated
only when a dated official source establishes it.

OPTIONAL UPGRADE GATE
One extra integration only, with a maximum total budget of 60 minutes, after the
complete cached journey works and before hour 10. Priority: an already-running
lab checkpoint that recognizes our IDs; otherwise a single BioMCP article/study
lookup. Keep its result behind the existing candidate/data interfaces. If no
checkpoint recognizes the target vocabulary, skip it. Semsimian semantic matching
is deferred unless a teammate already has a working ontology setup. No training,
full PrimeKG import, broad database integration or dependence on the lab repo.
The prototype works without the RTX GPU or the optional integration.

DEFENSIBLE DIFFERENTIATION
Disease maps, resource catalogues, evidence tracing, research roadmaps, outcome-
measure adaptation and partner matching already exist. Our prototype connects
these into one inspectable, proposed-use-specific journey. Users can see why a
resource surfaced, what is supported for their use, which publication matters,
what needs checking, who could resolve it, and a sourced proposal to approach them.
This is a demonstrated workflow contribution, not a world-first scientific claim.
The STXBP1/SYNGAP1 shared framework is a known validation case. A graph-derived
proposed lead is labelled as a lead, not an unrecognized discovery or treatment.

PASS CONDITIONS AND SUBMISSION
Demonstrate three scenarios: documented shared-framework route; additional
unknown SCN2A applicability of that same framework; a governed-resource route
where access requirements create a concrete researcher-collaboration task.
Also show one graph-generated candidate research lead with sources/qualifiers.
Use 6-10 frozen source/rule questions to inspect citation support, pending claims,
target/context mismatch, source aliasing and withdrawal. Include unknown queries,
DOI IDs containing '/', API/model failures and export of current state. Existing
software tests passing is not clinical validation. Synthetic conflict examples
may be used in tests but must not masquerade as real contradictory literature.
Measure manual and app-assisted preparation on the same source set and report
the actual result. Name the larger milestone: reaching an expert-reviewed
feasibility decision about adapting a natural-history framework. Explain the
baseline/proposed route and assumptions behind possible 10x acceleration; do not
equate faster brief preparation with a proven faster study launch or cure.
Deliver a working publicly hosted prototype, source repository,
README with architecture/setup/data reproduction/attribution/limitations, team
video and one-minute walkthrough. Save a repeatable genuine OpenAI output.

CUT ORDER IF ACTUAL TIME IS SHORTER
Cut optional integrations and variant enhancement first, then decorative graph
effects and extra metadata depth. Keep at least the known-framework case, honest
SCN2A gap, concrete public collaborator, evidence inspection/withdrawal, actual
OpenAI use, export and submission package. Protect the final 2-3 submission hours.
Defer extra diseases/goals, funding/investor scouting, drug recommendations,
automated trial eligibility, patient accounts/data collection, direct outreach,
multiuser editing and server-side PDF generation.
