PROBLEM
A rare-disease patient organization may find related diseases, publications and
research teams without knowing whether a particular resource can serve its own
study goal. Documented use, cohort fit, permissions and local capacity are
different questions. A promising connection alone does not settle them.

OUR USP
Assess a specific resource for a specific target and proposed use, then turn its
unresolved checks into a sourced collaboration proposal. Each result separates
recorded support, unknowns and pending source review. A user can hide a publication
and inspect exactly which checks depended on it; the exported brief preserves
that current evidence state, practical questions and public collaborator route.

PROTOTYPE SCOPE AND JOURNEY
We cover STXBP1-, SYNGAP1- and SCN2A-related disorders with one goal: preparing a
natural-history study, which tracks how a condition changes over time.
The user searches a disease/gene/symptom/organization, follows an explained research
connection, chooses a resource and opens its scoped checks. The published
STARR/ProMMiS framework is our known STXBP1/SYNGAP1 anchor, not a new discovery.
SCN2A applicability to that framework remains unanswered. A governed SYNGAP1
dataset case demonstrates access requirements and finding an appropriate partner.

The animated graph, patient/simple and doctor/expert views, source inspector,
evidence hide/reset and current-state text/PDF brief form one inspectable workflow.
Account/profile and appointment/discussion controls are clearly labeled local
previews; they do not authenticate a cloud account, book a doctor or send outreach.

ARCHITECTURE AND TRAINED MODEL
React/TypeScript and SVG/d3-force serve the interface. FastAPI serves search,
graph, assessments, sources and AI endpoints on the same public origin. Pinned
DisMech records and an additive curation overlay provide source-linked graph
context. A deterministic assessment engine controls scoped checks/outcomes.

The existing pretrained Claude Sonnet 4.6 model (claude-sonnet-4-6) extracts pending
claims from supplied public text, answers questions about the exact current view,
explains checks and proposes research hypotheses using recorded graph edges and
sources. We did not train or fine-tune a graph foundation model. Known routes
remain labeled; biological leads and extracted claims stay pending and are never
auto-imported into reviewed evidence. Output records genuine live/cached provenance.
Source-binding guards constrain identifiers and recorded state, not medical truth.
Claude use follows the team's reported organizer approval of Claude credits.

SOURCE INTEGRITY AND VALIDATION
DOI and PMID aliases identify one paper; repeated claims do not inflate study count.
Hiding the framework paper changes two supported checks to unknown while the
overall result can remain needs_information. An already-unknown check is not
counted as newly changed. Source hiding changes a view, not graph records or biology.

The current review log contains 58 rows: 20 human sign-offs preserved and 38 pending.
We do not claim that all contacts or biological assertions have been reviewed.
Imported AI-curated annotations, candidate inference and model hypotheses retain
their qualifications. DisMech attribution/license and pinned snapshot reproduction
are documented. No restricted participant data or measurement instrument is supplied.

Tests establish software/evidence bookkeeping, not clinical validity.

NOVELTY BOUNDARY
Disease graphs, resource directories, partner networks and measurement adaptation
already exist. Our contribution is their integration into an inspectable,
proposed-use workflow: candidate connection -> resource-specific assessment ->
source-dependence test -> unresolved checks -> sourced collaboration action.
We claim this demonstrated workflow and its review behavior, without claiming
exclusive worldwide novelty or a newly discovered disease connection.

MEANINGFUL MILESTONE AND CONDITIONAL 10X AMBITION
The intended milestone is an expert-reviewed feasibility decision about adapting
a research framework or accessing relevant research data. A brief is preparation
for that decision, not the decision itself.

No human timing pilot or measured 10x result exists yet. The functional timing
page starts empty and supports two teammates in reversed order on identical
sources/task/fictional constraints, with quality review, all failures and raw
CSV/JSON logs. It measures brief preparation only.

For a conditional milestone scenario, let D include discovery, preparation and
follow-up work; let r be unchanged partner-response/expert-review time.
Baseline time is D_manual + r; app time is D_app + r.
A tenfold reduction requires:
  (D_manual + r) / (D_app + r) >= 10
  D_manual >= 10 * D_app + 9 * r

Purely illustrative threshold, not observed evidence: if D_app=1 unit and r=1
unit, D_manual must be at least 19 units to produce a 10x milestone ratio.
These are abstract hypothetical units, not claimed hours or measured outcomes.
If review/response dominates and is unchanged, faster drafting may produce only
a small milestone improvement. Real stakeholder estimates and actual quality-
controlled measurements are needed to establish whether 10x is achievable.

IMPACT CLAIM
RareBridge aims to reduce repeated discovery and make the first expert conversation
more focused by exposing evidence gaps and preparing concrete next questions.
We have not measured faster feasibility approval, study launch, treatment
development or clinical benefit. Proposed reuse requires expert and access review.
